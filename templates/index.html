About I-FEST²

Science, discovery, and friendship—hosted in Tunisia.

I-FEST² is the International Festival of Engineering Science and Technology in Tunisia, organized by the Tunisian Association for the Future of Sciences and Technology (ATAST).

The festival brings young innovators together to present their work, exchange ideas, meet new cultures, and learn from one another. Beyond the competition, participants experience Tunisia through shared activities, visits, and excursions.

Eight fields of discovery

Explore our project categories

Find the field that best fits your research, invention, or creative work—and bring your idea to I-FEST².

01

Physical Science

Explore matter, energy, motion, and the laws that shape our universe through observation and experiment.

02

Environmental Science

Investigate natural systems and develop practical responses to environmental challenges.

03

Social Science

Study people, communities, identity, and the forces that shape how societies grow and change.

04

Computer Science

Create algorithms, software, and digital systems that turn complex problems into useful solutions.

05

Engineering

Design, build, test, and improve technologies made to solve real needs in the world around us.

06

Life & Biology

Examine living systems, health, behavior, biodiversity, and life’s relationship with its environment.

07

Multimedia

Combine image, sound, animation, video, and interaction to communicate ideas in powerful new ways.

08

Mathematics

Use patterns, models, logic, and proof to describe questions clearly and reveal new possibilities.
